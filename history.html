Breed background

The Corgi story.

Pembroke Welsh Corgi

The Pembroke Welsh Corgi is one of the oldest herding breeds in the world and is closely associated with Wales. These dogs were historically used to herd cattle, sheep, and other livestock, using their quick feet and sharp instincts to move animals without startling them.

Their compact bodies and athletic movement made them ideal for working in tight spaces, and their intelligence helped them handle tasks with independence and control.

Cardigan Welsh Corgi

The Cardigan Welsh Corgi is the older of the two corgi breeds and is named after the county of Cardiganshire in Wales. While similar in appearance, the Cardigan tends to have a longer tail and a sturdier build than the Pembroke.

Both varieties share a long working history and are admired for their confidence, alertness, and strong bond with their families.

“Corgis may be small in size, but they carry a remarkable history of diligence, loyalty, and working intelligence.”
