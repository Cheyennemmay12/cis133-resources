Care and wellbeing

Happy corgi, healthy corgi.

Exercise

Corgis are active, clever dogs that benefit from regular walks, playtime, and mental stimulation. Daily exercise helps prevent boredom, which can lead to unwanted barking or chewing.

Grooming

Their double coat needs consistent brushing, especially during shedding seasons. Good grooming routines help manage loose fur and keep the coat healthy and clean.

Training

Corgis are intelligent and eager to work, so positive reinforcement training works very well. Short, engaging lessons help them learn without losing interest.

What makes corgis thrive?

  • Daily interaction with their people
  • Time for both physical and mental play
  • A consistent daily routine
  • Regular health checkups and preventive care
