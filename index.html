Everything you need to love corgis.

Corgis are clever, affectionate, and full of personality. This site celebrates the charm of the breed and shares the stories, habits, and care tips that make them so memorable.

Why corgis?

Small dogs, big personality.

Smart

Corgis are known for being quick learners and high-energy problem solvers. They thrive when given jobs to do and enjoy staying mentally engaged.

Playful

Their playful nature keeps life fun and lively. From zoomies to silly greetings, a corgi can turn an ordinary afternoon into a comedy show.

Loyal

Many corgis form strong bonds with their people and love being near them. Their warm temperament makes them wonderful companions and family dogs.

Fast facts

A breed with a big legacy.

  • Corgis were originally herding dogs, bred to work closely with livestock.
  • They have expressive faces, short legs, and surprisingly athletic bodies.
  • Many corgis are affectionate, curious, and highly social with their people.
