Our purpose

Why I made this site.

For the love of corgis

This website was designed to celebrate one of the most lovable dog breeds on earth: the corgi. Their expressive faces, playful minds, and strong bonds with people make them fascinating subjects to learn about.

I chose corgis because they combine charm, history, and personality in a way that makes them easy to admire and fun to explore. They are a perfect topic for a website because there is both practical information and a lot of personality to share.
